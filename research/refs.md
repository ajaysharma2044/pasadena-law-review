# Reference teardown (Sept. 30, 2026)

Screenshots in this folder (1440 × 1100, captured with Firecrawl): `ylj.png`, `cornell-sun.png`, `cornell-political-union.png`.

## Yale Law Journal (yalelawjournal.org)

- Stack: Tailwind. Type: Cardo for headings, Open Sans for UI and body. Color: navy band #000F5C, link blue #4D98FF, white page. Corners: square.
- Layout: navy header with a crest and a letterspaced all-caps wordmark; search, newsletter and Subscriptions at the right. Nav: Print, Forum, Explore, Submissions, Masthead, About, Contact.
- Under the nav, a thin strip: "VOLUME 135 · NUMBER 8 · JUNE 2026 · VIEW MASTHEAD", with the numbers set larger.
- The featured piece is centered and text-only: a kicker ("ATKINS FEATURE"), the title in serif, the author, a 3-line abstract, the date and topic tags, then "View current issue." Below that, a Forum section of essays, each with an "ESSAY" kicker.
- **Take:** the volume strip; a centered text-only feature with an abstract; the split between the print journal and the Forum; Masthead and Submissions as top-level pages; footnotes and citation form.
- **Leave:** the navy and bright-blue palette, the crest, the carousel.

## The Cornell Daily Sun (cornellsun.com)

- Stack: Bootstrap. Type: EB Garamond for headlines, Roboto Condensed for the nav and labels, Inter for metadata. Color: red #830900 for the bar, buttons and labels; links #971927; text #2C2623; white page.
- Header: a red section bar with a hamburger menu, uppercase condensed section names with dropdowns, and search.
- Under the bar: the date and the Tip Line and newsletter buttons on the left, a blackletter nameplate in the center with "INDEPENDENT SINCE 1880" above it, and Merch, Donate and Advertise buttons on the right. A red rule runs under the header.
- Body: a red "Top Stories" label over a large photo lead. The "Opinion" rail sits in a light gray box, set off by a thin red vertical rule. Bylines use AP-style dates ("Tuesday, Sept. 29, 2026").
- **Take:** the red section bar; a centered nameplate with a small line above it; the date at left and actions at right; the red rule; red section labels; a rail set off by a vertical rule; AP-style dates.
- **Leave:** the blackletter nameplate (it is theirs), the ad banner, the donation popup, the row of social icons.

## Cornell Political Union (cornellpoliticalunion.org)

- Stack: Squarespace. Type: Garamond ATF Subhead for headings and nav, Helvetica Neue. Color: red #951100, an off-white page, a dark gray-brown band.
- Layout: a round seal logo, a serif nav (Home, About Us, Caucuses, Events, Join, Support Us) and red circular social icons.
- The hero is a collage of photos from real events: wood-paneled rooms, a speaker at a lectern. Below it, "Who We Are" with a red "Learn more" button over faded columns, then a dark "Speaker Events" band.
- **Take:** Events and Join as top-level pages; a plain statement of who they are; photos of their own people at their own meetings (once we have some).
- **Leave:** the seal pastiche, the faded columns, the "Learn more" button, the collage.

## Why all three read as real, and what we have to match

1. **Real names and dates everywhere:** bylines, volume and number, event dates. We use real content where it exists and bracketed placeholders where it doesn't. We never invent any.
2. **Photos of their own people.** We have none, so the site ships text-only. Real photos from the first meeting are the biggest thing that would make it look established.
3. **A wordmark with some history behind it.** Ours is a typographic nameplate.
4. **Ordinary site furniture** (dates, a section bar, a volume strip) instead of a designed landing page with a hero section.
5. **A shared red** (the Sun's #830900, the Union's #951100). Our #8B1A12 sits in the same family without copying either one.
