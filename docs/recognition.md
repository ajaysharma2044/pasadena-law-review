# Contributor recognition

Admin-managed honor badges: Published Author, Senior Contributor, Editorial Board, Featured Scholar, Founding Contributor. An administrator may grant or revoke at /admin/badges; actions are audited. Neither awards nor public display alter site access or club office.

Public display is opt-in under Account. Without opt-in, awards are only visible to the member and administrators. Do not treat a publishing byline as consent to reveal club membership.

## Constitution guardrails

Source: PCC_Law_Journal_Club_Constitution (Drive document, September 18, 2026; newer non-RED edition).

- Membership open to currently enrolled PCC students; no GPA, application, attendance or participation minimum; $0 dues; confidentiality upon request (Article II).
- President also serves as Editor-in-Chief; officer elections/terms and vacancies follow Articles III-V. Admin awards are not appointments or elections.
- The Board may appoint non-officer section editors and submissions coordinators (Article III), but only after the actual authorization; a badge isn't evidence of appointment.
- Editorial Committee reviews, edits and verifies sources; faculty advisor reviews articles for academic accuracy (Articles VIII-IX).

Administrative awards are *discretionary*, must reflect verified work, and may be revoked. Before using the Editorial Board badge, verify a real appointment. Do not advertise any institutional endorsement beyond what PCC formally approves.

Important: the older RED draft has a different officer list. Confirm which constitution was adopted before displaying any office titles. These badges do not constitute constitutional amendments.
