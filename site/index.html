A Student Proposal for a Restorative and Developmental Path in the Student Conduct Process

A proposal by [Author name]

I am proposing that Pasadena City College add a Restorative Resolution option to its student conduct and academic-integrity process, so that first-time and lower-level cases can be resolved through reflection and support rather than punishment alone. The option would cost the college little and fits within students’ existing rights under California law. It can start as a one-year pilot, and serious cases would stay in the formal process.
