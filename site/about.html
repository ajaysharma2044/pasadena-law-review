About the Review

The Pasadena Law & Policy Review is a student journal at Pasadena City College. It publishes essays, policy proposals, and notes by PCC students on law and public policy, and its members meet in person to debate them.

Law reviews are usually edited by law students. This one is edited by community college students, and much of what it covers concerns the rules that govern PCC and the other California community colleges.

Independence

Opinions in the Review belong to their authors, not to the editors, Pasadena City College, or the Pasadena Area Community College District.
