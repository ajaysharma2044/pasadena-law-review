The Review publishes writing by current Pasadena City College students on law and public policy. Faculty, staff, and alumni may write for the Forum.

What we publish

Essays
An argument about a legal or policy question. 3,000–8,000 words.
Proposals
A specific change to a rule, policy, or procedure, with the language you want adopted. Put the change in the first paragraph. Any length.
Notes
A close reading of one statute, case, or campus policy and how it works in practice. 2,000–5,000 words.
Forum pieces
A reply to something we published or debated. Up to 1,500 words.

How to submit

Email the piece as a Word file or a Google Docs link. Put the kind of piece and a working title in the subject line, for example “Proposal: parking citation appeals.” Include your name and your major. We don’t need your student ID number.

To find out whether an idea fits before you write it, send a paragraph describing it.

Citations

Cite sources in footnotes rather than a works-cited page. We follow The Bluebook. If you haven’t used it, cite as clearly as you can and the editors will fix the form during the cite-check.

After you submit

  1. Two editors read the piece and recommend accepting it, asking for revisions, or declining it.
  2. If it is accepted, one editor works with you on revisions.
  3. Editors check every footnote against its source.
  4. The piece appears online and is collected into the number at the end of the term.
